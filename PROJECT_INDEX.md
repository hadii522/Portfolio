## Publication decisions

- Employer and client systems: describe transferable engineering work; retain proprietary code, credentials, client identifiers and datasets privately.
- Classification experiments: keep historical results labeled; establish dataset provenance and repeatable evaluation before distributing raw data or claiming a benchmark.
- Unfinished prototypes and duplicate coursework: retain locally rather than presenting them as completed products.
- Third-party projects: retain their ownership and licenses; exclude them from original-work claims.
